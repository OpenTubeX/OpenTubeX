New features
• Follow conferences on media.ccc.de, search its talks, and watch them like media from the other supported platforms. Conference links open their page in the app.
• Subscription imports can sort channels into groups, from group names after the channel title in a .csv file or from the folders of an .opml file. The group editor can also select or clear all channels matching a search.
• The app font setting now includes Libron, a serif font designed for comfortable reading.
